About the firm

A trusted name in accounting & auditing, in the heart of Kotte

N B G Nanayakkara & Co is an accounting and auditing practice based at 1 1/1 Samudradevi Mawatha, Ethul Kotte, Kotte — minutes away from Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, the administrative capital of Sri Lanka. From our offices here we look after the books, audits and statutory compliance of a growing family of more than 40 clients.

Our approach is simple: accurate work, delivered on time, explained in plain language. Whether you run a family business, a growing startup or an established company, you deal directly with the people who know your file — not a call centre.

  • Accounting, auditing & tax services under one roof
  • Listed with CA Sri Lanka as a training organisation
  • Practical, plain-language advice for owners and directors
  • Easy to reach — three direct phone lines & client parking
